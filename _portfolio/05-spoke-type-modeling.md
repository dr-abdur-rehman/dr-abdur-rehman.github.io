Spoke-type rotors concentrate magnet flux, which makes them attractive for weaker ferrite magnets. However, the magnetic scalar potential of the rotor poles can oscillate, affecting machine performance.

**Highlights**

- Contributed to the analytical investigation of magnetic scalar potential oscillation in spoke-type PM flux-modulation machines (*Energies*, 2023).
- Contributing to the extension of this model to **spoke-type PM Vernier machines with alternate bridges**, which improve rotor mechanical robustness (manuscript in preparation).

**Methods:** analytical magnetic modeling, FEA verification.
