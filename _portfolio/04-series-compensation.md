Increasing the number of slots per pole per phase raises the torque density of a Vernier machine, but it also raises reactance, which worsens the power factor. This work applies **series compensation** to break that trade-off.

**Highlights**

- Supplied the required reactive power from an **auxiliary inverter with a floating capacitor**, enabling high-torque-density designs without the low power factor penalty.
- Designed and compared Vernier machines with different slots per pole per phase using FEA (*International Journal of Applied Electromagnetics and Mechanics*, 2020; IEEE ECCE, 2020).

**Methods:** analytical modeling, phasor analysis, FEA, drive-level compensation concept.
