Manual FEA is too slow to explore large design spaces. I build automated frameworks that generate, simulate, and evaluate machine models with minimal manual work.

**Highlights**

- **MATLAB–Ansys Maxwell co-simulation framework** with VBScript-based automated model generation for parametric design and optimization.
- **Parallel FEMM–MATLAB environment** (in development) for license-free FEA that runs many simulations at once, generating training data for **AI-based surrogate models**.
- Two-tier workflow: fast FEMM-based exploration, then high-fidelity verification in Ansys Maxwell.

**Methods:** MATLAB, VBScript, Python, FEMM, Ansys Maxwell, surrogate modeling.
