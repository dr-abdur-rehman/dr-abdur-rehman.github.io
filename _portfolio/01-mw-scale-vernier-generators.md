collection: portfolio
---

Direct-drive wind turbines avoid the gearbox but need very large, heavy generators because of their low speed. PM Vernier machines can deliver much higher torque density, but their low power factor makes them hard to use at MW scale. This research addresses that trade-off.

**Highlights**

- Designed and analyzed a **15 MW surface-PM Vernier generator** for direct-drive wind turbines (*Energies*, 2023, Feature Paper).
- Proposed the **spoke-type PM Vernier machine** as a promising candidate for MW-scale direct-drive applications (ICPE-ECCE Asia, 2023).
- Conducted a comparative design of flux-modulation topologies for a **20 MW** direct-drive generator (Ph.D. thesis).
- Developing an **analytical design framework under explicit power-factor constraints** for high-power PM Vernier generators (manuscript under review).

**Methods:** analytical sizing, slot/pole selection, air-gap and power-factor analysis, electromagnetic FEA (Ansys Maxwell).
