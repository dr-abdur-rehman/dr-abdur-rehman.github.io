Rare-earth magnets are expensive and face supply risks. Ferrite magnets are cheap and widely available but much weaker. Flux-modulation (Vernier) machines can compensate for this, making ferrite designs competitive.

**Highlights**

- Developed a **novel equivalent magnetic circuit model** for consequent-pole ferrite-magnet Vernier machines, which use less magnet material (*IEEE JESTPE*, 2022).
- **Comparative analysis and design optimization** of ferrite-based surface-PM Vernier machines (*Energies*, 2024).
- Designed a **low-cost PM Vernier machine** with improved electromagnetic performance (IEEE CEFC, 2024).
- **Experimental validation:** tested ferrite consequent-pole and ferrite surface-PM Vernier prototypes to verify analytical and FEA predictions.

**Methods:** equivalent magnetic circuit modeling, FEA-based optimization, prototype testing.
