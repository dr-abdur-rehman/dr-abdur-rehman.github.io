Electric propulsion for urban air mobility (UAM) needs motors with very high torque and power density. This project evaluated whether PM Vernier machines can meet these demands in a direct-drive configuration.

**Highlights**

- Designed and evaluated PM Vernier machines against application-level torque, power, and efficiency requirements for UAM propulsion (*IEEE Canadian Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering*, 2025).

**Methods:** requirement-driven sizing, electromagnetic FEA, performance comparison.
