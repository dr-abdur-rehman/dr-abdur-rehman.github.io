**February 2016 – August 2017**

- Delivered hands-on workshops for undergraduate students on embedded systems and power-electronics projects, combining short lectures with practical lab demonstrations.
- Supervised and mentored semester and final-year student projects, guiding students from design through hardware implementation and testing.
- Topics included microcontroller programming (Atmel 89C51, PIC18F, Arduino), inverter and power-supply design, and practical measurement and testing.
